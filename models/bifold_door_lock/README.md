# Bifold Door Lock

A 3D-printable lock that slips over the top of a bifold door pair to hold the doors together. A front knob lifts it off. The defaults fit 1-7/16" doors.

## Features

- The slot is exactly the door thickness, open at the top and both ends, with a floor that leaves the top gap above the door.
- A lead-in chamfer at the slot mouth guides the lock onto the doors.
- The lock edges are rounded and the knob has a front groove for a fingertip.
