# Corner Cover

A 3D-printable L-shaped guard that covers the sharp outer corner of cabinet trim, such as the plinth of interior cabinetry. The defaults fit a 1-1/4" corner and are 5-9/16" tall.

## Features

- The pocket is open on two sides, so the guard slides onto a corner from the top or presses on from the side.
- The two walls cover the corner, and the outer corner is rounded.
- The top and bottom edges are lightly rounded.
- Set the height, outside and pocket widths, and the corner and edge radii.

## Printing

- **Material**: PETG; TPU gives a snug, forgiving fit on slightly uneven trim.
- **Orientation**: Standing upright as modeled, or on a side for the strongest layers along the walls.
- **Fit**: Set Inner_Width to the trim's thickness plus about 0.2 mm.
